**Native economic layer for xMesh/NovaNet, AI agent swarms, and sovereign innovation.**

## Executive Summary

QCoin implements a multi-rune, capability-bearing economic system on QNET that aligns incentives across decentralized infrastructure, autonomous AI agents, prototype development, and immersive creative practice.

Runes are not passive tokens. They are active coordination instruments that carry utility, governance rights, and narrative weight.

The economy is live-orchestrated by **Nexus** — the central (yet non-dictatorial) intelligence layer running on the Hannover node.
